Inventory GUI Maker

Vanilla-style container textures with slot grids, machine slots, progress widgets, player inventory and hotbar.

Grid

Size

Extra height goes above the player inventory, giving placed items room.
256 is what blit assumes; larger sizes export the matching blit call. Filled widget sprites are packed into the free space.

Placed slots

Choose a tool above the preview and click to place. Drag to move; ←↑→↓ nudge (Shift = snap step), Del removes, Ctrl+Z undoes. Indexes continue after the grid.

Progress widgets

Arrows, flames or your own sprites. The empty version is drawn on the panel; the filled one goes to free canvas space, and the exported Screen reveals it by progress.

Custom sprites

Filled PNG required; the empty version defaults to a grey silhouette. Removing a sprite removes its widgets.
